Honest limits

The model has genuine holes — the middle of the strip is thinner than the ends, with gaps where no two views had valid depth. Nothing was invented to fill them. Dense depth reached 32.6% of measurable pixels vs a 50% target (forward-looking camera: ~25% of each frame is sky). The flight is near-collinear, so the GPS residual is a lower bound on true metric error.
